Welcome to Greenwood Nature Sanctuary

Preserving living botanical heritage, fostering biodiversity, and providing a peaceful sanctuary for wildlife and human connection with nature.

Featured Exhibit: The Victorian Glasshouse Conservatory

The centerpiece of our sanctuary is the historic Glasshouse Conservatory, an architectural marvel constructed in 1894. Today, it safeguards hundreds of endangered subtropical flora and towering palm specimens under its soaring glass arches.

A Living Sanctuary for Generations

Nestled across 300 acres of protected woodland, meadows, and wetlands, Greenwood Nature Sanctuary serves as an essential ecological buffer for regional wildlife. We are committed to passive conservation, allowing natural succession to replenish habitats while offering thoughtfully designed walking trails for visitors of all ages.

Our scientific team collaborates with universities and global conservation institutions to monitor pollinator populations, propagate threatened native flora, and restore natural water filtration systems through wetland marsh reclamation. Every visit directly funds our non-profit habitat stewardship initiatives.
